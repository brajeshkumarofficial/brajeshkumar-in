Research

Research & Analysis

Areas of research focused on systematic observation of financial-market behaviour.

01

Market Structure

Study of swing structure, trend development, reversals, continuation and changing market context.

02

Price Behaviour

Observation of how price reacts around important levels, ranges, trends and structural zones.

03

Momentum

Study of momentum conditions and their relationship with price movement across multiple timeframes.

04

Time-Based Analysis

Research into time relationships between market phases, price movement and potential structural changes.

05

Historical & Quantitative Research

Testing observations against historical data to identify repeatable relationships and probabilities.

06

Chart-Based Research

Visual representation of research observations using charts, trendlines, structure and other analytical markings.

Research Process

  1. Define the market behaviour or hypothesis to be studied.
  2. Identify relevant price and time variables.
  3. Study historical examples and market context.
  4. Test the observation and record both supporting and invalidating conditions.
  5. Document the research conclusion and limitations.

Research methodologies and future publications may evolve as additional evidence becomes available.
