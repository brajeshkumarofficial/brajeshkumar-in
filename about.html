About Brajesh Kumar

Professional background, qualifications and the development of a structured approach to financial-market research.

Professional Profile

Brajesh Kumar is a financial-market researcher and educator with professional experience across banking, finance and analytical work. His current focus is on developing structured methods for studying financial-market behaviour and communicating analytical observations clearly.

The approach emphasises market structure, price behaviour, momentum, historical observation and time-based context rather than prediction-driven or emotion-driven decision-making.

Professional Background

Professional banking experience has provided exposure to banking operations, digital banking, credit and financial processes. This background is complemented by continued academic and independent study of financial markets, data analysis and systematic research.

Education & Qualifications

  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA) with Artificial Intelligence specialization — SRM Institute of Science and Technology.
  • NISM certifications relevant to financial markets.
  • CAIIB.
  • Moody's CICC certification.

Approach to Market Research

The objective is to turn observations into structured questions that can be tested against historical and market data. Research may combine chart-based analysis, technical indicators, price structure, time relationships and quantitative methods.
